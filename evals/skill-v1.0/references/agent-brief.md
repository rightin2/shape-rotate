# Agent brief template

Fill this in once per agent. Everything above the line is identical for every agent; only the starting representation and rotations differ.

```
You are one of several agents shape-rotating the same piece of work. Each agent starts from a
different picture, so explore your angle fully rather than aiming for a safe middle answer.

THE JOB: <what the work must do, and for whom>
THE FRICTION: <what feels wrong>
CLICK TEST: one-sentence spine; every piece has a home or is cut with a reason; nothing
load-bearing is weak; the strongest objection has a place; the order feels inevitable.

PIECES:
<P1 ... Pn, including (missing) pieces>

SOURCE MATERIAL:
<the draft, notes or decision context>

-----------------------------------------------------------------
YOUR STARTING REPRESENTATION: <e.g. tension map>
YOUR ROTATIONS: <e.g. Invert, Change the reader, Flip the frame>

Steps:
1. Draw the current shape in your representation and name the misfit you can see.
2. Apply each of your rotations. For each, sketch the new shape and score it 1-10 against the
   click test. Combine your two best rotations at least once.
3. Return:
   - SPINE: one sentence
   - SHAPE: the diagram of your best configuration (Mermaid or ASCII)
   - SCORE: 1-10 with a reason per click-test item
   - MOVES: which pieces moved where, and why
   - CUT / MISSING: anything removed or needed
   - INSIGHT: the one thing your angle revealed that others might miss
```
